[![62560a8728700f4911e76c59 Grouparoo acquisition](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/605e01bc25f7e19a82e74788/62560a8728700f4911e76c59_Grouparoo%20acquisition.png)](https://airbyte.com/blog/airbyte-acquires-grouparoo-to-accelerate-data-movement)

[**Airbyte acquires Grouparoo**](https://airbyte.com/blog/airbyte-acquires-grouparoo-to-accelerate-data-movement)

Grouparoo, an open-source reverse-ELT product, joined the Airbyte team this week. We’ve brought on a team of reverse-ETL experts in-house, which is a huge step in our ability to accelerate data movement for our users!

[**Read more here >>**](https://airbyte.com/blog/airbyte-acquires-grouparoo-to-accelerate-data-movement)

[**Airbyte Cloud is now available**](https://cloud.airbyte.io/signup)

We launched Airbyte Cloud for anyone looking for the same extensible, data movement solution that Airbyte offers, without the resources or expertise required to manage an Airbyte open-source deployment.
