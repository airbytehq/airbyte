## Introducing Airbyte Plus

[![Introducing Airbyte Plus](https://airbyte.com/images/community/newsletter/bodies/external/oct-2025-introducing-airbyte-plus/69005b1978334fc9eabf0ff3-unnamed-4-3c31710ad6.png)](https://airbyte.com/blog/airbyte-plus)

Airbyte Plus is a new pricing plan designed for growing teams looking for core functionality with the predictability of annual pricing and accelerated support.

Plus is identical to the Standard plan but offers annual billing instead of monthly usage-based pricing, eliminating cost fluctuations and making it easier to plan data infrastructure budgets for the year.

The plan also includes accelerated support with prioritized response times, perfect for small and mid-sized teams running mission-critical data pipelines who need extra support at a reasonable price.

Same product, same features, just predictable costs and faster support when you need it.

[Learn more...](https://airbyte.com/blog/airbyte-plus)

## Snowflake Destination: Now Faster and Cheaper

[![Snowflake Destination](https://airbyte.com/images/community/newsletter/bodies/external/oct-2025-introducing-airbyte-plus/airbyte-plus-snowflake-v6-white-01k8rt3wm14mpjbfagxck8qsq7-9beea4ee7e.png)](https://airbyte.com/blog/snowflake-destination-enhancements)

This massively improves scaling as time-sensitive pipelines and dashboards get dramatically faster refresh rates at a fraction of the cost. These optimization principles are being rolled out across other destinations to make data movement so fast and affordable that cost and latency stop being concerns.

## Apache Polaris Support for S3 Data Lakes

[![Apache Polaris Support](https://airbyte.com/images/community/newsletter/bodies/external/oct-2025-introducing-airbyte-plus/apache-polaris-01k8rv1h2p3r92xcr1mst77fz9-fe840bc22c.png)](https://docs.airbyte.com/integrations/destinations/s3-data-lake#polaris)

Airbyte now supports Apache Polaris catalog integration in its S3 Data Lake Destination, bringing open table format management directly into your data pipelines.

This means you can write data to S3 while automatically registering tables in Polaris, enabling seamless interoperability across your data lakehouse tools like Spark, Trino, and Flink.

For teams building modern data architectures, this eliminates the manual catalog management overhead and ensures your S3 data is immediately queryable and discoverable across your entire stack.

[Read our docs.](https://docs.airbyte.com/integrations/destinations/s3-data-lake#polaris)

## MSSQL Speed Updates

[![MSSQL Speed Updates](https://airbyte.com/images/community/newsletter/bodies/external/oct-2025-introducing-airbyte-plus/airbyte-plus-mssql-01k8rtkm5c94zr5c11448mc6gd-e916f579e8.png)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/micheltricot_mssql-syncs-with-airbyte-just-got-84-faster-activity-7386802810035732480-lMHS)

Airbyte's MSSQL connector just got a massive performance upgrade, delivering 84% faster syncs after migrating to the new CDK with built-in speed mode. As Airbyte's third-largest database source by volume, SQL Server is now also one of their fastest connectors.

The enhancement is rolling out to customers soon. If you're syncing SQL Server data at scale, expect dramatically faster pipeline performance.

[Read our CEO's post on it here.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/micheltricot_mssql-syncs-with-airbyte-just-got-84-faster-activity-7386802810035732480-lMHS)
