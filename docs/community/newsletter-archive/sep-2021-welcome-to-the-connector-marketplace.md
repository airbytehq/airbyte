[**Welcome to the Connector Marketplace!**](https://discuss.airbyte.io/c/connector-marketplace/10/none)

A lot of companies wants to pay to get their custom connectors built, and we know lots of consulting agencies and contractors that wants to help. So we built a Connector Marketplace! A note: if you contribute a new connector to the open-source repo, we'll give you $500 worth of Airbyte Cloud credits!

[**Check the marketplace >>**](https://discuss.airbyte.io/c/connector-marketplace/10/none)
